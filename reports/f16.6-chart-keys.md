chart: it need not be a word or read as English. Eleven verdicts closed a space only because the forced key was not a
word, and several more because it was not English. This page reads each record again with the key free and asks the
item's question: does K4 fit more settings than its stand-ins? Every number below is from the experiment's own
`raw.json`; nothing was rerun. Where the record kept the count against copies, the item is decided by count. Where it
did not, the space with the key free is named and its verdict taken from the experiment that decides that space.
