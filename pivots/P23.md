after 0267"). On the Priorities page, F15's table sits before the family tables with open rows (F1.13, F1.14, F6.1), so

## What happened before

- 0253-0255 and 0267 decided every hand key schedule K4 fits, on one free alphabet and on two, by English against copies.
  In both, K4 sat inside its stand-ins (p 0.51, p 0.43).

  finds a third to two thirds of plants, and K4 behaves like its copies. The cells are short of checks, not of compute.

model of what K4 says (F15.1). Write drafts under it (F15.2). Then measure, on held-out plants, whether the drafts as a
prior find more plants than the 24 letters alone (F15.3, bar: at least 2 more of 12) before any K4 run.
