# F14.6: procedures of up to three hand steps from K1-K3's operations

Item F14.6 (`order-of-work.md`, issue #93): "every procedure of up to three hand steps built from K1-K3's operations
(keyed alphabet, table with a key word, K3's rotation, keyword columnar, reversal), keys from the source list, ranked by
the length of their description. Write the grammar and count the procedures first; skip combinations an earlier family
already closed and cite them." Written 2026-10-28, before any run.

## The five operations

| Symbol | Operation | Where it comes from | Key |
|---|---|---|---|
| M | keyed monoalphabet: the message rewritten through a keyword-mixed alphabet | K1, K2's KRYPTOS alphabet | a word |
| T | the table with a repeating key word (Vigenere, Beaufort or variant; plain and cipher alphabets standard or KRYPTOS) | K1, K2 | a word, a start |
| R | K3's rotation: write in rows of w, turn the grid (or Stehle's rule a + b i mod 97) | K3 | a width or two numbers |
| C | keyword columnar | K3 (KRYPTOS as column key) | a word |
| V | reversal | Sanborn's mirrored tableau | none |

R, C and V are moves (permutations of places). Keys come from the source order in `program.md`: the 171 puzzle words of
0115 (K1-K3 words, the vocabulary, the 2025 letter's words), then the dictionary.

## How compositions collapse

1. A move followed by a move is a move (0129's type-1 library holds single moves; 0134, 0158, 0160, 0162 compositions).
2. M next to T merges into T's plain or cipher alphabet (M then T = Quagmire with a keyword plain alphabet; T then M = a
   keyword cipher alphabet).
3. T followed by T is one T whose key is the letter-by-letter sum of the two key streams (period lcm(m, n)).
4. M followed by M is one M; M alone is a simple substitution, excluded by the known letters (S at 33 and K at 74 fixed,
   letter clashes, `04-structure.md`).
5. A move alone is excluded by letter counts (three E's needed, two carved; 0051).
6. M and a move without T (substitution and transposition) is F9.1, closed.
