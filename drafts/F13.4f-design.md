puzzle words x starts (about 1,750) x dictionary rotations (about 1.8 million) x 24 rules: about 7.6e10 settings per
text, too many. Plan:
1. For each rule (3 modes x plain/cipher/key alphabet standard or KRYPTOS), puzzle word and start: subtract the puzzle
   word's stream; the remainder is a periodic key of the dictionary word's length n (3-24).
2. Per n, each column's shift by letter frequency (exact, 0234/0235's screen; C), about 2.3e9 ops per text.
3. Keep settings whose screened key, or the key after 0234's coordinate-ascent climb, is a rotation of a dictionary
   word of length n (dictionary rotation index as in 0115); score those readings by MIX8m (0233).
4. Statistic as 0234 (max z over n against 100 climbed shuffles); plants (random puzzle word + random dictionary word)
   against 10 own shuffles at n = 5, 10, 15, 20. Power will follow 0234: decided to about n = 15-20.
Reuse: drafts/attack-f13.4a-0234.py (climb, MIX8m loader), 0115's word lists (SPACED, EXTRA, vocabulary).
