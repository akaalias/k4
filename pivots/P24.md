# From the move libraries to simple hand methods

## Why we pivoted then

Alexis asked for a step back (#123): since nearly the start the work had been in territory that is hard to do on
paper. Two fresh-eyes reviews followed, one of the experiment log only and one of the success criteria against the
official sources only (#124). Alexis then paused F14.5b (0274, 192 of 932 blocks saved, `drafts/F14.5b-attack.py`),
held F6.2 (about 116 h and $70 on AWS), revised the criteria and queued F16 ahead of both.

## What happened before

- Experiments 0001-0118 were mostly exact, cheap, hand-sized tests.
- 0019 set aside Sanborn's one-to-one NYPVTT=BERLIN clue because the width-21 repeats looked stronger. 0168 later
  found no statistic of 71 outside every shuffle; the move-first families were not revisited.
- 0136 showed that free-alphabet cells cannot be decided from 97 letters; about 25 runs tried statistical deciders on
  them anyway, and about 22 runs chased edge cells that all resolved as chance.
- About 11 verdicts closed a space only because the forced key was not a word (0244, 0247-0250, 0271-0273, 0115, 0241,
  0095, 0049). Under the revised criteria a key may come from a chart, so these are open on K4 alone.
- Every exact test used EAST (22-25), which is second-hand; none tolerated EAST being wrong.
