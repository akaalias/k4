# From keyed periodic keys to a running numerical key (Gromark)

## Why we pivoted then

Family 1 was re-entered for four experiments (0009-0012) and came out bounded for periodic, progressive and autokey keys under every alphabet the puzzle's vocabulary could build. Nothing in it was left that the known letters could decide cheaply.

## What happened before

- 0009 and 0010 read the key letters forced by EASTNORTHEAST and BERLINCLOCK as English, under the standard, KRYPTOS and 248 vocabulary-mixed alphabets: the best stretch equals the best from scrambled copies.
- 0011 ruled out the progressive key at periods 1-25 and 31-51 at every step.
- 0012 chained plaintext autokey from the known letters: lags 1-25 and 30-52 dead, 26-29 force noise.

## The change we believed was needed

The known letters force 13 and 11 consecutive key shifts. If those shifts were digits from a number stream that feeds on itself, a Gromark-style recurrence, the stream would show it directly. That is cheap to test and needs no word list (0013).
